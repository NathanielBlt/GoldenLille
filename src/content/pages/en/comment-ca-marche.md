---
title: "How it works"
---

Golden Lille is a real-world trail through the city, played with a physical deck of cards — this site is just the map, not the game.

Each card in the deck hides a QR code. Scan it on location and you'll land on a short page for that exact spot: a bit of history, a photo, nothing more. The riddles, the letters, the rest of the ritual — that all stays on paper, in your hands.

This site won't tell you where to go next. Wandering is part of it.
